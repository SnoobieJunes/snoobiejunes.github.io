What it does

A quiz that pays attention

Most quiz apps ask everyone the same questions. Schlr rates you and every question on the same scale — like chess ratings — and picks each next question so you're stretched, not stuck.

It finds your level

Every answer nudges your rating and the question's. The quiz stays hard enough to teach you something, easy enough that you keep going.

Every question is fact-checked

A second, independent AI pass has to confirm each question before it counts. If the checker isn't sure, the question is thrown out.

Explore mode

Productive doomscrolling. Read a short lesson checked against real sources, get quizzed on it, then follow a thread to the next topic. Repeat until bedtime.

An honest report card

See where you stand across topics. Acing hard questions counts for more than a streak of easy ones.

Private by design

The AI runs on your iPhone, or on Apple's Private Cloud Compute. Prefer your own model? Point Schlr at any endpoint — the key never leaves your Keychain.

A shared question bank

Good questions get pooled, so every device gets them instantly — even older phones that can't run the AI, even offline.

How it works

Type a topic. That's it.

Pick anything

"Roman aqueducts." "Baking chemistry." "The Meiji Restoration." If you can name it, you can study it.

Answer questions

The quiz calibrates as you go and locks onto your level. Keep going as long as you like — or fall down an Explore rabbit hole.

Fix what's broken

Bad question? Flag it or swap it. Your feedback feeds the shared bank, so the whole thing gets better as people use it.

The fine print, up front

Honest about the AI. Boring about your data.

Questions are AI-generated — and we say so

The app discloses it. The reason to trust it anyway: nothing is published unless an independent fact-check stands behind it.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking

No sign-up. No analytics. No third-party code at all. The only thing that leaves your device is anonymous question content for the shared bank.

Be an early Schlr

Early testers shape the question bank everyone else inherits. Your flags and answers literally make the app better.
