Under the hood

Questions worth arguing over

Trivia lives or dies on its questions. Triviant's come from the same engine as Schlr, our adaptive learning app — which means every one is independently fact-checked before any player ever sees it.

Your Wheelhouse shapes the game

Tell Triviant what you're into, and your interest profile tilts the question mix toward ground you and your opponents actually care about.

Difficulty that matches the match

Questions carry real difficulty ratings sharpened by crowd answers, and your career rating steers what you're served — close games, not blowouts.

Invent a topic tonight, own it tomorrow

In 1-on-1, both players' prompts blend into a fresh generated topic. Those questions get fact-checked and banked — tonight's inside joke becomes part of the shared question pool.

Fair by design

Lockstep rounds, heartbeat liveness so silent disconnects can't stall a match, and forfeit rules that keep ranked play honest.
